3. Secure In-App WebView Browsing

When you open services such as DBT Status Tracker (https://pfms.gov.in/SitePages/DBT_StatusTracker.aspx) or Know Your Payment (https://pfms.gov.in/SitePages/KnowYourPayment_Dw_NewNew.aspx), the app renders the official government webpage inside Android's system WebView with strict security controls:

  • ✓
    Strict HTTPS-Only Enforcement: Cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled (usesCleartextTraffic="false") and mixed content is blocked.
  • ✓
    Verified Official Domain Allow-List: In-app navigation is restricted strictly to verified official PFMS domains (pfms.gov.in) to prevent untrusted external redirects.
  • ✓
    Standard Session Cookies: Temporary cookies required by the official PFMS portal for CAPTCHA verification are managed locally by Android's WebView CookieManager and are never exported.

4. Android Device Permissions Used

The application requests only two standard, non-invasive install-time network permissions required to connect to the PFMS portal:

android.permission.INTERNET

Internet Connectivity

Required to load live official PFMS web pages and service portals over secure HTTPS connections.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Network Status Check

Used to detect whether Wi-Fi or mobile data is available so the app can display a helpful offline message if disconnected.

5. Advertising, Google AdMob & User Consent

To support ongoing maintenance as a free public-utility application, this app integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display interstitial advertisements at natural navigation breaks between screens.

  • ✓
    Non-Intrusive Placement: Ads are shown only at natural transitions between pages and never interrupt active form filling, CAPTCHA entry, or payment status checks.
  • ✓
    User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent: Where required by regional privacy laws, consent is gathered using Google's official User Messaging Platform before personalized or non-personalized ads are requested.
  • ✓
    Advertising ID Controls: Google AdMob may use the standard Android Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for frequency capping and fraud prevention. Users can reset or delete their Advertising ID anytime under Android Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Children's Privacy: This application is designed for general citizens verifying public financial and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) records. We do not knowingly solicit or collect any data from children under the age of 13.

Policy Updates: We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect security enhancements or Google Play policy updates. The "Effective & Updated" date at the top of this document always indicates the latest revision.
